The Story Behind This Guide

While designing covers for my books, I sometimes struggled to describe exactly what I wanted AI to create or to preserve particular details during editing.

I began taking notes about what I learned through these experiments with lighting, framing, composition, style, and image editing. Those notes and visual comparisons eventually grew into the five-volume The Language of Visuals guide.

You can also follow the development of AI image tools and the changes in how I work with them at ademyuce.tr.
